A 3D interactive beach scene built with Three.js featuring a sandy shore meeting a turquoise ocean under a real daylight skybox. The world includes a tugboat sailing in the distance, a whale tail breaching, a pod of 5 dolphins jumping across the ocean in V-formation, a hot air balloon, airplane, kite, rainbow, ferris wheel, rocket, sandcastle with bucket and shovel, volleyball court with ice cream cart, beach towel scene, and a brick-textured lifeguard hut with a glowing RectAreaLight window. Use W/S/A/D to move the camera, Q/E to move up/down, mouse drag to look around, scroll to zoom. Click the toggle button (top right) to switch between Orbit Mode and Walk Mode.

Wow Point — Pod of 5 Jumping Dolphins:

Five real 3D dolphin models (Dolphin.glb) swim in V-formation across the ocean — just like the airplane flies across the sky. Each dolphin leaps in and out of the water using a sine wave animation offset by phase: Math.sin(t * 2.0 + i * 0.8) * 12 - 6. The -6 offset shifts the wave below Y=0 so dolphins genuinely dive underwater between jumps, creating a natural staggered leaping effect across the ocean surface.
