CSE160 Assignment 3 — Creating Virtual World

A 3D interactive village built with WebGL featuring a horse that walks autonomously through a brick village with collision detection. The world includes apple trees, golden hay patches the horse eats, blue water pots, flying birds, and fluffy clouds in a blue sky. Use W/S/A/D to move the camera, Q/E to rotate, mouse drag to look around, B for bird's eye view, F to add blocks, and G to delete blocks. Blocks are added with random sizes and heights shown by different colors.

Features:

  • 32x32 voxel world with brick textured walls (4 height levels)
  • Animated horse with walking legs, wagging tail, and mane
  • Horse collision detection with walls and trees
  • Trees
  • Hay patches — horse eats them and they respawn
  • Blue water pots along the path
  • Flying birds circling at tree level
  • Fluffy white clouds in the sky
  • Add blocks (F) with random size/height, Delete blocks (G)
  • Bird's eye view (B key)
  • Mouse drag + keyboard camera controls
  • FPS counter
